## About

Made by Spacely Digital. Questions, credit corrections or removal requests: spacelydigital@gmail.com.

For fun and general information only. Positions can be late, incomplete or wrong, and many aircraft are not shown. Not for navigation, air traffic, safety or emergency use.

Airline, operator and aircraft-manufacturer names are trademarks of their owners, used only to identify aircraft. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any airline, manufacturer or data provider.

## Data

Aircraft data: adsb.fi, https://adsb.fi (scan the code on this page).

Flight routes: ADSB.lol (Open Database License).

Maps: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, © OpenStreetMap contributors (Open Database License).

Aircraft photos: Planespotters.net. Each photographer is credited beside their photo.

Satellite imagery in the U.S.: USGS The National Map.

Satellite imagery elsewhere: Sentinel-2 cloudless – https://s2maps.eu by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016 & 2017).

Place search and time zones: Open-Meteo (GeoNames data, CC BY 4.0).

First location guess: GeoJS. This product includes GeoLite2 data created by MaxMind, available from https://www.maxmind.com.

U.S. street addresses: U.S. Census Bureau geocoder. Other addresses: Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
