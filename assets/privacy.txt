## What is sent

To show planes near you, this app sends your approximate location, rounded to a few miles, to public flight and map services (adsb.fi, OpenFreeMap, Open-Meteo, USGS, EOX). It looks up the planes it shows with ADSB.lol and Planespotters. Your first city guess comes from your internet address (GeoJS). What you type in search goes to Open-Meteo, the U.S. Census Bureau or OpenStreetMap to find the place. Every service also sees your TV’s internet address. Your settings and history are saved only on this TV. No accounts, ads or analytics. You can erase it all in Settings.

## What is saved on this TV

Your location, the place or address you chose, your settings, the planes seen today and the saved maps. Nothing is sent to the developer.
