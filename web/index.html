Before we start

To show planes near you, this app sends your approximate location, rounded to a few miles, to public flight and map services (adsb.fi, OpenFreeMap, Open-Meteo, USGS, EOX). It looks up the planes it shows with ADSB.lol and Planespotters. Your first city guess comes from your internet address (GeoJS). An address you search for goes to the U.S. Census Bureau or OpenStreetMap to find it. Your exact location, settings and history stay on this TV. No accounts, ads or analytics. You can erase it all in Settings.
