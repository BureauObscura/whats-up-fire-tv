## What stays on this TV

Your exact location, the address you typed, your settings, the planes seen today and the saved maps stay on this TV.

## What is sent

To show planes near you, the app sends your approximate location, rounded to a few miles, to public flight and map services. Searches go to geocoding services. See the screen shown before first use for the full list.
